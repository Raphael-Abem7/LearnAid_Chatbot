About

This web app serves as a study assistant which aids Ghanaian Senior High School students with their personal studies. All responses generated from this app are solely based on the GES-approved curriculum learning materials, thus its knowledge base is within the learning materials used by public SHS schools. The AI models used to generate responses are local Large Language Models (LLMs) and for that matter do not require internet connection in order to function. The AI model selected for this web app (Qwen 2.5:1.5-instruct_q5_K_M) is of a size of GB, and is capable of running on any computer with a minimum of 6GB. It is my strong belief that this web app will be able to aid students with their personal studies with ease.
